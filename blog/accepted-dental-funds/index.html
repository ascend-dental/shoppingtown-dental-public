Accepted Dental Funds at Shoppingtown Dental

10 June 2024 · Shoppingtown Dental

At Shoppingtown Dental, one of our core beliefs is that every person should have access to affordable dentistry. We understand the confusion that comes with different payment and financing options, so we encourage you to contact our friendly team if you’re ever unsure about your coverage or need help navigating your health fund.

We can demystify the process because we work with these health funds every day. Keep reading to learn more about the funds we accept and how you can finance your dental treatment.

What Dental Funds Do We Accept?

We want our patients to know we accept all private health funds. We also process every dental claim through HICAPS, making claiming quick and easy — so you only pay the gap (if any) on the day. Because we partner with every available health fund, you can receive the maximum rebate possible.

We know it can be confusing with so many funds, which is why we do our best to keep our fees low and help you navigate your cover, including:

  • Medibank — one of Australia’s largest insurers, offering comprehensive dental options and an extensive provider network, with a focus on preventive care.
  • Bupa — customisable dental cover with a long list of providers and gap-free check-ups on eligible policies.
  • HCF — a member-owned fund that encourages preventive care, with gap-free dental check-ups for eligible members.
  • NIB — several dental insurance options for individuals and families, with flexible coverage and a large provider network.

Questions About Affordable Dentistry in Doncaster?

If you need more information or have questions about the dental funds we accept, we’d be happy to help. We want you and your family to receive quality, affordable dental care — call us on (03) 9848 2399 with any questions.
