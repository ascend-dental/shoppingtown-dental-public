About Shoppingtown Dental

Patient-centred dental care in the heart of Doncaster.

At Shoppingtown Dental in Doncaster, we pride ourselves on a patient-centred experience that puts you at the heart of everything we do. Our experienced dentists are committed to being the best at what they do, and our team ensures your visits are comfortable and pleasant.

Because we listen carefully to your oral health concerns, we can organise treatment plans tailored to your unique needs — including your budget, timeline and lifestyle. The Shoppingtown Dental team looks forward to serving your oral health needs with friendliness and compassion.

Our Triple-A Philosophy

The promise behind every visit

Always Accessible

We design treatment plans around your unique needs so you always have access to high-quality dentistry and expert care — conveniently located in Westfield Doncaster.

Always Affordable

Dental costs matter. We accept all private health funds, offer flexible financing, and can even help you access your superannuation when needed.

Always Available

Our hours reflect our commitment to being there for you — including Saturday and evening appointments, plus a 24-business-hour booking guarantee.

The Ascend Dental Difference

Shoppingtown Dental is part of Ascend Dental — led by a team of experienced dental industry leaders committed to the best possible patient care. Our clinics are individually managed with a personal touch, ensuring top-quality service for every patient. We prioritise our patients with our signature Triple-A Philosophy and patients-first approach, backed by a wide range of dental services including treatment under general anaesthetic in a private hospital setting.
