Teeth Whitening

A Brighter, More Confident Smile

Request a Call

Teeth Whitening in Doncaster

Stubborn stains from coffee, tea, red wine, dark berries or soy sauce are no match for professional teeth whitening. At Shoppingtown Dental, we offer safe, effective whitening that’s tailored to your smile and supervised by your dentist — far more powerful and reliable than over-the-counter products.

Your Whitening Options

  • In-chair whitening — a professional treatment that lifts stains and brightens your smile in a single visit, perfect before a special occasion.
  • Custom take-home kits — professional-strength gel and custom-fitted trays let you whiten gradually at home, at your convenience.

Whitening That Suits You

At your consultation we check that your teeth and gums are healthy, discuss your goals and recommend the best approach for you — helping you achieve a natural-looking, brighter smile while keeping sensitivity to a minimum.
