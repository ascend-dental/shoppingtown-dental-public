Sleep Dentistry

Relaxed, Anxiety-Free Dentistry

Sleep Dentistry in Doncaster

Dental anxiety shouldn’t stand between you and a healthy smile. At Shoppingtown Dental, we offer a general anaesthetic option so nervous patients — and those needing more complex treatment — can receive the care they need in complete comfort.

The Benefits of Sleep Dentistry

  • Comfort for anxious patients — sleep peacefully through your treatment
  • Ideal for complex care — complete multiple procedures in one visit
  • Helpful for a strong gag reflex — relax while we work
  • A positive experience — remove the stress that keeps many people from the dentist

Is It Right for You?

Your comfort is our priority. During a consultation, our Doncaster dentists assess your health and treatment needs and discuss whether sleep dentistry is the right choice — so you can get the care you need without fear or stress standing in the way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sleep dentistry can help patients with significant dental anxiety, a strong gag reflex, complex treatment needs, or those undergoing multiple procedures who'd prefer to sleep through it all.

When appropriate, general anaesthetic is administered and monitored by a qualified anaesthetist in a suitable facility. We assess your health carefully and discuss whether it's the right option for you.

Under general anaesthetic you're fully asleep and won't remember the procedure. You'll wake once treatment is complete, with someone to drive you home to recover.
