Crowns & Bridges

Restore Strength and Appearance

Request a Call

Do you struggle with broken, decayed or missing teeth? At Shoppingtown Dental in Doncaster, we see how these challenges impact daily living, from difficulty smiling confidently to the inability to eat the foods you enjoy most. Our expert dentists in Doncaster are here to help you find solutions to regain your confident, comfortable smile and offer high-quality dental crowns and bridges.

What Is a Dental Crown?

A crown is a tooth-shaped shell of porcelain, ceramic or other durable material that covers your entire tooth down to the gum line. Its purpose is to strengthen the tooth, restore function and leave you with a natural-looking tooth restoration.

When Do You Recommend a Crown?

Some examples where our Doncaster dentists recommend a dental crown include strengthening a tooth that is too damaged or decayed to repair with a filling and protecting a tooth after root canal treatment. Crowns are extremely strong and durable and long outlast any filling. Our material is colour-matched to neighbouring teeth to ensure it blends naturally with your smile.

When you visit our Doncaster dental clinic, our dentists will assess your needs to determine if a crown is right for you. If there is another option, they will present it to you so you have all the facts you need to make an informed treatment decision.

Getting a dental crown typically requires two visits to Shoppingtown Dental. The first involves taking X-rays if necessary and removing some tooth structure to create room for the crown. We then take impressions and place a temporary crown on the tooth to protect it while your custom restoration is being fabricated.

At the second appointment, we will check your custom-crafted crown for fit and cement it securely on your tooth. With excellent oral hygiene and routine check-ups and cleans, your new crown can last ten years or longer.

How Does a Dental Bridge Work?

If you have one or two missing teeth in a row, a fixed bridge is an excellent solution. We place crowns on the teeth on either side of the gap, and an artificial tooth (or teeth) sits in the missing tooth space to complete your smile.

Getting a dental bridge follows the same steps as a crown and usually requires two appointments. This type of tooth replacement is an excellent option if you don't want a dental implant or removable partial denture, and it can last 5–10 years when cared for properly.
