Root Canal Therapy

Save Your Natural Tooth

Root Canal Therapy in Doncaster

When decay or injury reaches the soft inner pulp of a tooth, it can cause infection and significant pain. Root canal therapy removes the infected tissue, cleans and seals the tooth, and lets you keep your natural tooth rather than losing it.

At Shoppingtown Dental, we use modern rotary endodontic techniques to make treatment as comfortable, efficient and precise as possible.

Signs You May Need a Root Canal

  • Persistent or severe toothache, especially when chewing
  • Lingering sensitivity to hot or cold
  • Swelling or tenderness in nearby gums
  • Darkening of the tooth
  • A recurring pimple on the gum

What to Expect

Under local anaesthetic, your dentist removes the infected pulp, thoroughly cleans and shapes the canals, then seals the tooth. In most cases we recommend a crown afterwards to protect the tooth and restore full strength and function. The result is relief from pain and a natural tooth you can keep for years to come.

Frequently Asked Questions

Modern root canal treatment is carried out under local anaesthetic and is no more uncomfortable than having a filling. In fact, it relieves the severe pain caused by an infected tooth.

Treatment is usually completed over one or two appointments, depending on the tooth and the extent of infection. We'll give you a clear timeline at your consultation.

A treated tooth is often restored with a crown to protect it and restore full function. With good care, a root-canal-treated tooth can last many years.
